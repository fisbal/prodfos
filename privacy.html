ProDFOS · by MartinB

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

In short: ProDFOS has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Your documents stay on your device. The only time document content leaves your device is when you ask DocMind™ AI a question — and then it goes directly to the AI provider you chose, using your own API key.

What ProDFOS stores

  • Your documents are read from where you keep them. Files you create (merged, split, scanned, edited or filled PDFs) are saved on your device — in Download/ProDFOS on Android, Documents\ProDFOS on Windows, or inside your browser when you use the web app.
  • Your library (recent files, last page read) and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Your AI API key, if you add one, is kept only on your device.

We (the developer) never receive any of this. There is no ProDFOS server to send it to.

DocMind™ AI

DocMind is optional. When you use it, ProDFOS extracts the text of the open document and sends it, with your question, directly from your device to the provider you selected in Settings — Anthropic or OpenAI — authenticated with your own API key. That provider's privacy policy applies to that request: Anthropic, OpenAI. Nothing is sent unless you open DocMind and run a request.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to scan documents when you choose Scan.
  • Photos / files — only the files and images you pick.
  • Internet — only for DocMind requests you make. The Forms engine runs a private connection inside the app on your own device (127.0.0.1); it is never reachable from the network.

Web app

The web version at fisbal.github.io/prodfos is hosted on GitHub Pages, which delivers the app's files only. Documents you open there stay in your browser's own storage on your device and are never uploaded. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

ProDFOS does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Settings → Clear Recent Documents removes your library. Uninstalling the app (or clearing your browser's site data for the web app) removes everything ProDFOS stored. Files you created stay in your folders until you delete them.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: github.com/fisbal/prodfos/issues.
